Izuna Crescentia: Consistent Multi-AI Character Craft

Izuna is a multi-creative project centered around Izuna Crescentia, an original anime character developed across diverse AI workflows achieving nearly 90% visual consistency without flaw.

Beneath the Moonlight Ecosystem

Serving as the primary creative umbrella, Beneath the Moonlight unites every multimedia branch born from the Izuna concept into one cohesive creative universe.

Among its core branches are Izunamix—a complex media player software engineered to automate music playback and audio cues for live gatherings and events—and Clyrove Maid Diary, an ongoing creative diary channel thriving under Beneath the Moonlight.

Beneath the Moonlight connects character identity, automated event audio tooling, and daily storytelling into a single living universe.
